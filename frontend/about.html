ABOUT SAGRIVISION AI

Information for farmers, presented clearly.

SAgriVision AI is intended to help farmers explore seed information and crop options through accessible AI-supported tools.

THE PURPOSE

Support questions from seed to crop choice.

The project brings together an informational Supported Seeds page, image-based Seed Identification, and Crop Recommendation based on selected farm conditions.

  • Start with the public Learning and Features pages.
  • Sign in before using protected application tools.
  • Review the inputs and outputs carefully.
RESPONSIBLE USE

AI information needs farmer judgment.

Results may be affected by image quality, available classes, selected inputs, local conditions, and the limits of the underlying data.

For high-cost decisions, unusual results, crop health concerns, or uncertain identification, consult a qualified agricultural professional.

KEEP LEARNING

Understand the tools before relying on them.

The Learning page explains the current workflows and how to read result language without treating it as a guarantee.
